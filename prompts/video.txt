Cozy lofi anime girl studying late at night, Studio Ghibli cel-shaded painterly style. She sits at a wooden desk by a tall rainy window, warm desk-lamp pooling golden light over an open notebook, a steaming mug of tea, and a stack of books. Headphones on, pencil drifting across the page. Outside the window: soft city lights blurred through rain, distant neon signs glowing pink and blue, occasional cars passing. Inside: rain streaks tracing down the glass, steam curling lazily from the mug, lamp light flickering, pages turning, a sleeping cat curled at her feet, the gentle sway of a wind chime. Slow drifting camera — gentle push-ins on the notebook, a soft pan across the desk, a quiet rack focus from the window to her face. Mood shifts with the music: introspective and calm in the verses, dreamier and more saturated in the choruses, with bokeh, light leaks, and falling cherry blossom petals drifting through the room. Warm amber and teal palette. Painterly, hand-drawn texture throughout. Cinematic, atmospheric, nostalgic.
